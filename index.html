Our Story

Mobile pop-up pizza shop

Noah and his mom and dad started this pop-up pizza shop to better serve Barron County — specifically Chetek, Wisconsin. In Polish, babka means grandma.

They wanted a pizza company that truly values the ingredients in their dough, toppings, and everything in between. Babka's uses only the highest-quality ingredients, and that plus the care each of them puts into the work is what makes the difference. Come taste the difference.
